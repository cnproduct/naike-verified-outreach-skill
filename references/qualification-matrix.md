# Qualification matrix

## Positive evidence

### Silicone baby and kitchenware

Strong terms include silicone baby feeding, toddler tableware, weaning, suction bowl, bib, food-grade silicone, bakeware, kitchenware, food container, and private label. Stronger evidence is a product catalog or wholesale page showing several relevant SKUs.

### Promotional products and corporate gifts

Strong terms include promotional products, branded merchandise, corporate gifts, corporate swag, custom drinkware, giveaway, event merchandise, distributor, importer, wholesale, and private label. A real agency/distributor page or catalog is stronger than a search snippet.

### General importers and retailers

Look for supplier, wholesale, trade, distribution, brands carried, vendor, private label, or buyer/procurement language. A consumer storefront alone does not prove willingness to source from a new factory.

## Hard negatives

Reject or hold for review when the primary business is a directory, government/education institution, personal page, parked domain, blog/content farm, casino/gambling, gaming, crypto/financial service, medical/legal service, restaurant/hospitality-only business, unrelated software/service agency, or a company whose products conflict with the chosen Naike lane.

## Evidence quality

1. Official product/wholesale page with company identity and contact path.
2. Official About page plus product/catalog evidence.
3. Official domain with consistent product and company metadata.
4. Reputable professional/company profile that matches the official domain.
5. Search snippet or directory listing only: discovery signal, never sufficient for auto-send.
