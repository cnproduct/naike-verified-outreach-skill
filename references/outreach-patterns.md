# Outreach patterns

## First message

1. Greeting using a verified contact or team.
2. One sentence reflecting the verified business model.
3. One relevant Naike capability, tied to the product lane.
4. One validation question about a current or upcoming sourcing project.
5. A low-friction next step such as sharing a small set of relevant options.

## Follow-up sequence

- Day 0: concise, evidence-based introduction.
- Day 3–4: one short bump asking whether the category or project is relevant.
- Day 7–10: add one relevant proof point or sample option only if it matches the verified lane.
- Stop on reply, unsubscribe, bounce, complaint, or clear disinterest.

## Do not do

- Do not say “I noticed you are looking for…” unless the source proves it.
- Do not claim a company is a distributor, importer, or buyer from a generic email alone.
- Do not send silicone, drinkware, gifts, and general merchandise pitches together.
- Do not use a fixed pain-point paragraph for every company.
